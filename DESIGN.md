# DalelCheck design contract

A review-focused evidence dossier: ordered published requirements, the full fixed source and a selected human finding share one working surface. Burgundy rules, white evidence pages, serif source reading and monospaced requirement codes establish its own identity. This replaces the inherited blue table cabinet under the owner’s explicit 26 October direction; the earlier source/screenshot documents remain historical evidence.

## Screen contract

The worksheet collection opens a dossier index with actual filled/total finding counts and explicit saved-report state. Source texts, publications and reports have compact records focused on their frozen content. Requirements/checklist editing and findings collections retain secondary native tables.

Inside a worksheet, native requirement buttons select a requirement without a write. The centre pane shows the entire immutable source with exact quote slices marked by their saved UTF-16 boundaries. The right pane shows both authored RU/KK requirement texts, human verdict, finding version/owner, rationale, quote coordinates and exact quoted text. A “Показать цитату в источнике” button locates the first quote and focuses the scrollable source. No innerHTML or normalization is used.

Only the worksheet author sees a finding-edit action while the worksheet is unarchived and unsealed. A saved report uses its own frozen finding snapshots, hides editing and labels the lock. The full published checklist remains available in a disclosure. The existing complete unsaved report preview and one-time report save retain their server revision, UUID, required closure rationale and stale-write refusal behavior. The saved full report, historical actor/time/note/digest and every requirement remain in the full-report disclosure.

A source mark proves textual containment only. “Не найдено” and “Конфликт” remain manual findings. complete describes the authored marks, without semantic, truth, legal or commercial approval. Only synthetic source texts are in scope.

## Tokens and typography

Page `#f8f5f4`, white surface, ink `#2e2427`, muted `#716568`, burgundy primary `#843449`, hover `#622135`, line `#e0d4d6`, source highlight `#f3df9e`. System sans serves controls, Georgia serves the source and title, system mono serves codes/coordinates. Two-pixel surface corners, four-pixel controls, flat borders and a top identity rule distinguish the dossier from the other workspaces. No new asset requests or dependencies.

## Responsive and access rules

At 1120px, the source and requirement rail remain side-by-side while the finding pane follows across the full width. At 740px the source and finding stack, the requirement chooser wraps; at 430px it becomes one column. Full source owns a labelled keyboard-focusable scrolling region; every quote can be located from the finding. Native controls are >=44px, inputs 48px; the archive checkbox label is >=44px. Authored language nodes, source line breaks and exact coordinate labels are preserved. Reduced motion removes transitions. Light evidence pages are the designed scheme.
